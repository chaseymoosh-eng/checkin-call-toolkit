Check-in CallsCheck-in Call Toolkit · friendly calls, not a therapy or crisis service

This is a friendly-call program, not therapy, counselling or an emergency service. Volunteers offer a listening ear. If someone is in danger, call your local emergency number.

🔒 Data is stored only in this browser on this device (localStorage). Nothing is sent anywhere. Use a password-protected device and export backups regularly. Clearing your browser data will erase it.

Loneliness check uses the 3-item UCLA Loneliness Scale (Hughes, Waite, Hawkley & Cacioppo, Research on Aging, 2004). Call approach inspired by Kahlon et al., JAMA Psychiatry, 2021.
